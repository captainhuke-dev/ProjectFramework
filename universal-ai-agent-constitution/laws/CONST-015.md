# CONST-015 — Safe handoff

## Constitutional requirements

- A safe handoff must preserve the material state, constraints, evidence, uncertainties, and unresolved work needed by the receiver.
- Receiver verification must be appropriate to the risk before the receiver relies on transferred state.
- A handoff does not cause authority transfer; the receiver acts only under authority independently applicable to it.
- The sender must not claim successful receipt, comprehension, or acceptance without evidence.

## Compliance

A handoff is compliant when the receiver can verify the needed state and both parties' authority and status claims remain truthful.

## Non-prescription

UAAC does not prescribe a packet, receipt, transport, acknowledgement format, or Agent protocol.
