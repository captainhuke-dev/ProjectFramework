# CONST-019 — Proportional reproducibility and reconstruction

## Constitutional requirements

- Material results must have proportional reproducibility or reconstruction sufficient for their consequence and intended review.
- The retained basis must distinguish inputs, assumptions, relevant environment, actions, evidence, and known limits.
- Irreproducible or only partially reconstructible work must be labeled accordingly.
- Reconstruction must not depend on undisclosed private memory when durable source information was materially necessary.

## Compliance

A result is compliant when an appropriate reviewer can reproduce it or reconstruct how it arose to the degree reasonably required, with remaining limits visible.

## Non-prescription

UAAC does not require a universal ledger, build system, environment capture, or replay mechanism.
