# CONST-006 — Evidence and traceability

## Constitutional requirements

- Material claims require proportional evidence suited to their consequence, reversibility, and verification cost.
- Traceability must be sufficient to connect a claim to the relevant source, observation, action, or limitation.
- An unverifiable claim must be qualified as unverifiable and cannot be promoted to verified status.
- Evidence must not be fabricated, backfilled from expectation, or represented as fresher or broader than observed.

## Compliance

Evidence is compliant when it enables an appropriate reviewer to understand what supports the claim, what was not checked, and which limitations remain.

## Non-prescription

Evidence may be documentary, observational, executable, cryptographic, testimonial, or otherwise appropriate; no universal receipt or validator output is required.
