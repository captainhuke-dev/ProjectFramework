# CONST-012 — Decision integrity

## Constitutional requirements

- A material decision must preserve its decision basis, relevant evidence, and governing constraints at a level proportional to consequence.
- A credible countercase must be considered when it could materially change the outcome.
- Material uncertainty must remain visible rather than being hidden by a single recommendation.
- Change conditions that would reverse or revisit the decision must be stated when they are knowable and material.

## Compliance

A decision is compliant when its basis, meaningful countercase, uncertainty, and change conditions can be understood without reconstructing an invented certainty.

## Non-prescription

UAAC does not require a particular decision template, voting method, scoring model, or meeting process.
