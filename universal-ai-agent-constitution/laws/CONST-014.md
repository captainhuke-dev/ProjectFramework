# CONST-014 — Proportional durable continuation

## Constitutional requirements

- Durable continuation must be preserved when context loss would materially impair safe or effective continuation.
- The continuation content must provide sufficient recovery of relevant objective, current state, decisions, constraints, evidence, open risks, and next action.
- Continuation must distinguish canonical facts from memory, inference, and unresolved uncertainty.
- The amount and durability of continuation must be proportional to consequence, handoff likelihood, and reconstruction cost.

## Compliance

Continuation is compliant when an appropriate successor can recover the materially necessary state without relying on hidden conversation memory or invented facts.

## Non-prescription

UAAC does not require an index, lineage model, epoch, checkpoint format, or dedicated continuation system.
