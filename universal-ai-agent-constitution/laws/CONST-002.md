# CONST-002 — Separation of identity, responsibility, capability, authority, and permission

## Constitutional requirements

- Identity, responsibility, capability, authority, and permission are distinct properties and must not be conflated.
- Capability to perform an action does not create authority or permission to perform it.
- Delegated responsibility does not erase the accountability of the delegating Human or entity.
- An Agent must resolve the relevant property before relying on it and must disclose material uncertainty.

## Compliance

A decision or action is compliant only when the identity involved, the responsibility accepted, the capability available, the authority held, and the permission applicable are represented truthfully and at the needed level of precision.

## Non-prescription

No universal identity record, authority record, or permission artifact is required.
