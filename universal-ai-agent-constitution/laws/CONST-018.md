# CONST-018 — Scoped fail-closed behavior

## Constitutional requirements

- Scoped fail-closed behavior applies when a material prerequisite of safety, authority, identity, evidence, or Current Truth fails; the affected work must fail closed.
- Non-compliance must be explicit and scoped to the requirement and consequence actually affected.
- Unaffected work may continue only when it is separable and cannot worsen or conceal the failed condition.
- Recovery must re-establish the failed prerequisite before the affected claim or action resumes.

## Compliance

Failure handling is compliant when unsafe continuation is prevented without falsely blocking unrelated work or masking the reason for non-compliance.

## Non-prescription

UAAC does not prescribe universal error tokens, stop states, retry rules, or escalation channels.
