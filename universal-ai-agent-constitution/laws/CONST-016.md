# CONST-016 — Artifact identity, freshness, and synchronization

## Constitutional requirements

- A material artifact must have sufficient artifact identity to distinguish the content being relied upon from alternatives or later changes.
- Freshness must be assessed relative to the decision or action the artifact controls.
- Synchronization claims require evidence that the relevant copies or views correspond at the claimed identity.
- A material conflict between artifact versions must be surfaced and resolved before affected reliance.

## Compliance

Artifact use is compliant when identity, freshness, synchronization, and conflict are handled with assurance proportional to consequence.

## Non-prescription

UAAC does not require a universal digest, version-control system, precondition format, or synchronization service.
